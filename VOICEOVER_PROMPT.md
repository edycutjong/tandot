Welcome to Tandot.
The trustless, AI-powered savings circle.
Tandas are the backbone of informal finance in Mexico, but 1 in 3 fail when the organizer runs off with the money.
Tandot eliminates the human organizer.
First, our GPT-4 AI agent evaluates your financial profile to calculate a dynamic trust score, ensuring you are matched safely.
Then, you contribute seamlessly via Bitso SPEI. Your funds are converted into MXNB and locked securely in an Arbitrum smart contract.
When it's your turn, the smart contract automatically executes the payout directly to your wallet.
Zero trust required. It's time to secure your savings.
